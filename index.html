A Place Where Every Stay Becomes a Memory

Coco Beach Hotel Jomtien Pattaya presents a sophisticated coastal retreat where modern design meets the essence of Thai living. Ideally located along Jomtien Beach Road between Soi 7 and 8, the hotel offers seamless access to the shoreline while remaining close to Pattaya’s vibrant attractions.
With 192 elegantly appointed rooms (218 bedrooms), each space is thoughtfully designed to embrace tranquility, featuring views of the sea and swimming pool. Inspired by the natural beauty of the ocean and enriched with Thai craftsmanship, every detail reflects a harmonious balance of modern comfort and cultural identity. Whether for leisure, family holidays, or corporate gatherings, Coco Beach offers an experience defined by comfort, elegance, and attentive service.

Coco Beach Hotel Jomtien Pattaya offers a thoughtfully curated lifestyle experience designed for both relaxation and recreation. From serene poolside moments to family-friendly spaces and wellness facilities, every element is crafted to enhance your stay by the sea.
