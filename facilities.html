Facilities Designed for Every Moment

At Coco Beach Hotel Jomtien Pattaya, every facility is designed to elevate your stay from relaxing poolside moments to family-friendly spaces and wellness experiences. Whether you seek leisure, comfort, or connection, our thoughtfully curated amenities ensure a seamless beachfront lifestyle.

Fin Bar

Fin Bar – A refined lobby cocktail bar featuring premium and imported beverages in a relaxed, sophisticated setting.

Bliss Pool Bar

Relax by the pool with refreshing beverages, tropical cocktails, and a laid-back seaside atmosphere at Bliss Pool Bar.

Restaurant At Siam

At Siam™ offers an all-day dining experience in a contemporary yet inviting atmosphere. Serving a selection of Thai and international cuisine, the restaurant accommodates up to 200 guests, perfect for both relaxed meals and social gatherings.

Swimming Pool

A spacious resort-style swimming pool designed for both relaxation and recreation. Surrounded by a tranquil setting, it offers the perfect escape for guests of all ages to unwind, refresh, and enjoy the tropical ambiance.

Fitness Center

Stay active throughout your stay at our modern fitness center, thoughtfully designed to support your wellness routine in a comfortable and relaxing atmosphere.

Kid club

A welcoming space designed for young guests to play, explore, and enjoy fun-filled moments in a safe and family-friendly environment.

We look forward to welcoming you to Coco Beach Hotel Jomtien Pattaya. Whether you are planning a relaxing getaway, a family holiday, or a special event, our team is here to assist you every step of the way.
